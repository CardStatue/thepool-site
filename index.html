Ownership

First one in owns the lane.

Your pool stays yours

You sign it from your own wallet and hold the position. Every trade that crosses it pays you, and you can pull out whenever you want.

Every coin after you pays you

Open a coin's pool first and you become its fee partner on chain. Every coin anyone launches against it earns you a share, for as long as it trades.

We never hold your money

We build the transaction and you sign it. No deposit, no vault, no key of ours anywhere near your funds.

Pool liquidity is not locked. Whoever mints a pool can withdraw their side of it at any time, including us on $POOLS. That is what makes the position theirs, and it is the risk you take when you buy a coin paired to a pool someone else owns. Lock status is public on chain — check it before you buy, on any pool here or anywhere else.

Keep the pool deep and the lane is yours. Let it dry up and it opens back up for someone else — coins already trading keep paying you either way.
